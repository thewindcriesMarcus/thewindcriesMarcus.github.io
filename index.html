02 — Selected work

What I built this year

Three projects from first year. Each one lists the tools, what I was responsible for, and what I would do differently next time.

Run Little Boy Run

Run Little Boy Run

A endless runner game, where the player dodges obstacles, lasers barriers and more! Whilst also collecting coins. Heavily inspired by Subway Surfers, Minion rush and other endless runners.

My role: everything — gameplay classes, spawning, scoring, HUD and the end-of-round state.

Kaidan

Unreal Engine 5 · Level design · Duo · 9 weeks

In 1973, around 4 miles outside of Takayama, there is an old estate that 19-year-old Keiji Yamada has just recently moved into, inheriting the home after his grandparents passed away, although they hadn’t lived in the estate for many years before they passed, the reason they abandoned this house being a complete mystery to Keiji. One night, Keiji drifts off to sleep and awakens to find something is off…something evil. Something evil and ungodly has cursed his home, and he must do whatever he can and whatever he must in order to survive and escape the curse that, for some unknown reason, wants Keiji dead. Old tales of monsters and curses he had heard have come to life and want to kill Keiji too, forcing Keiji to face off against demons and the supernatural in order to survive and escape the curse.

My role: layout, pacing, sightlines, playtest notes and iteration.

Christmas 2D Game

Christmas Piskel 2D Game· 8 weeks

A christmas themed game with hand-drawn assets, perfect for the winter season. First project of this course giving me experience to get familiar with Ue5

My role: modelling, UVs, texturing and engine import.

03 — About

About me

I am a first-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh campus. I came to the course from playing games and taking them apart, and I am here to learn to build them properly.

I am most interested in gameplay programming — the systems that decide how something feels to control. This year I have worked in C++ and Blueprints, modelled and textured my own assets, and recorded and implemented my own audio.

Next I want to go deeper into C++ and take on a longer team project.

Strengths
